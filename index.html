What we do

Ten finishing processes,
one building

BIC is able to complete complex jobs that require multiple finishing processes entirely in house — prepared and coated by hand, with the attention to detail critical jobs demand.

01

Sulfuric Acid Anodizing

Aluminum oxide build-up for abrasion and corrosion resistance, roughly 336 hours per ASTM B117. Available clear or dyed.

MIL-A-8625 · Type II
02

Hard Coat Anodizing

Where surface hardness and abrasion resistance are the main criteria. Far thicker build-up than standard anodizing, dyed or undyed.

MIL-A-8625 · Type III, Class 1 & 2
03

Powder Coating & Wet Paint

Commercial, industrial and architectural coatings prepared and coated by hand. Standard and custom colors, metallics, textures and gloss levels.

Polyurethane · Epoxy · C.A.R.C. · MIL Spec
04

Zinc Plating

Corrosion protection by barrier and sacrificial anode, without breaking down the substrate's surface integrity.

ASTM B633 · QQ-Z-325 Type I, II, III
05

Chemical Film Coating

Chromate conversion coating for aluminum — corrosion protection while keeping conductivity, and better adhesion for later paint.

MIL-C-5541 · Class 1A & Class 3
06

Passivation

Removes iron, lead and other contaminants, then accelerates formation of the passive layer that resists corrosion.

Citric & Nitric
07

Media Blasting

Compressed air and abrasive media to clean, deburr or prepare a surface. Aluminum oxide, glass bead and plastic media.

Restoration & surface prep
08

Silk Screening

Instructions, settings and branding printed directly onto metal or plastic parts — more durable than a label, and fewer parts to inventory.

Metals & plastics
09

Steel Fabrication

A full service steel fabrication shop on site with raw material in stock — handrails, custom BBQ grills, patio furniture repair.

In-house shop · Materials stocked
10

Fence Panel Fabrication

Large scale and custom wrought iron fencing, gates, bollards, guard rails and carports — powder coated in virtually any color.

Fabricated & coated in house
Why BIC

Jim Brooks started this shop in 1986. It is still run that way.

Brooks Industrial Coatings has been providing customers with metal finishing needs since 1986. Jim Brooks had a vision to offer his customers a quality product with great customer service — and that commitment to exceptional quality and customer service is what lets you know your projects are in reliable hands.

We work with clients all over the United States and with large manufacturers here in Austin, alongside local retail customers who need a single part done properly.

  • Every process handled in house — no parts shipped between shops

Specifications we work to

Buyers search for the spec, not the service name. Every standard already named on BIC's own service pages, in one place.

ProcessSpecificationDetail
Sulfuric anodizeMIL-A-8625 Type II0.0002″–0.0003″ build-up plus equal penetration. Clear or dyed — black, green, gold, red, blue. Approx. 336 hrs salt spray per ASTM B117.
Hard coat anodizeMIL-A-8625 Type III, Class 1 & 2Typical 0.001″ build-up and 0.001″ penetration. Class 1 undyed, Class 2 dyed. Color varies with alloy, champagne through dark gray.
Chemical filmMIL-C-5541 Class 1A / Class 3Chromate conversion on aluminum. Clear or golden finish. Approx. 168 hrs salt spray per ASTM B117. Maintains conductivity.
Zinc platingASTM B633 · QQ-Z-325Type I, II, III, Class 1, 2 and 3. Gold zinc, clear zinc (RoHS compliant) and black zinc.
PassivationCitric & NitricRemoves iron and lead contamination, accelerates the passive layer.
